Partnering with Indigenous Peoples

The Major Projects Office is committed to working in consultation and partnership with Indigenous Peoples and governments on all major projects. This includes meeting the Crown’s legal Duty to Consult with Indigenous peoples, and the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (UNDRIP) principles. The Office is advancing reconciliation in regulatory and permitting processes by upholding Indigenous rights, while incorporating Indigenous Knowledge into assessments, and ensuring Indigenous Peoples derive economic benefits from major project development.

Indigenous Advisory Council

The Indigenous Advisory Council supports the Major Projects Office by providing expert advice on policy and operational practices related to regulatory and permitting processes and advancing Indigenous economic participation with respect to major projects. The Council will help shape how the Office works with Indigenous Peoples, including providing advice on advancing economic participation in major projects, and how best to integrate UNDRIP principles throughout the process.

Funding opportunities

The Government of Canada is committed to removing barriers to the participation of Indigenous Peoples in major projects.

Capacity Funding

The Major Federal Projects Office is committing $40 million over two years to increase the capacity of Indigenous Peoples to engage on major projects early and consistently.

For more information, please contact us.

Indigenous Loan Guarantee Program

The Indigenous Loan Guarantee Program facilitates access to affordable capital to Indigenous groups. Initially launched with $5 billion in loan guarantee authority focused on the energy and natural resources sectors, the Government of Canada doubled the Indigenous Loan Guarantee Program to $10 billion and extended its scope to major projects across all sectors of the economy, apart from gaming, in March 2025.

For more information on the Indigenous Loan Guarantee Program, including the application process and eligibility requirements, please visit www.cilgc-cgpac.ca.

Work with Us

Are you a member of a First Nation, Métis, or Inuit government, or designated representative of a community with interests or concerns related to major projects? The Major Projects Office is here to listen and work with you on your priorities with respect to major projects. We will:

  • Ensure your perspectives are considered early and throughout project decision-making
  • Coordinate across federal departments so you don’t have to repeat your input
  • Help navigate federal programs that support the participation of Indigenous Peoples in major projects and support economic development
  • Honour your governance system and any relevant protocols, including treaties, self-government agreements, and consultation protocols

If you have any priorities or concerns related to major projects, or questions for our team, please contact us. We are committed to responding in a timely manner.
